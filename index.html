TaroBird

A workspace for the AI agents you run on your own computers.

TaroBird lets your phone and computers share the same Bots and conversations. Command-line agents run on a host that you install and control; the TaroBird apps are clients for that host.

Optional Google connections let your configured tools work with mail, calendars and documents at your request. Google connections require separate setup and permission; browsing a Google website does not grant API access.
