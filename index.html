三步开始Get started

装好电脑端，手机扫一扫就能用Install on your computer, scan once with your phone

01

安装桌面端Install the desktop app

在 Mac 或 Windows 上安装 TaroBird，设为主机，新建 Bot 并选择电脑上已装好的 AI 引擎。Install TaroBird on your Mac or Windows PC, make it your host, and create a Bot with an AI engine already installed on that computer.

02

手机扫码配对Pair your phone

在电脑上打开「添加设备」显示二维码，手机扫一下，再在电脑上确认 8 位配对码。Open Add device on the computer to show a QR code, scan it with your phone, then confirm the 8-digit code on the computer.

03

随时随地派任务Work from anywhere

同一 Wi-Fi 直接连；外出时通过 Tailscale 或你自己部署的中转服务器连回电脑。Connect directly on the same Wi-Fi; when you are out, reach your computer through Tailscale or a relay server you run yourself.

功能Features

多个 Bot，一个对话，进度一目了然Several Bots, one conversation, every step visible

手机、平板和电脑共用同一套 Bot 与对话。群聊里让不同的智能体分工协作；任务完成或需要你确认时，手机会收到通知。Your phone, tablet and computers share the same Bots and conversations. Let different agents split the work in a group chat, and get a notification when a task finishes or needs your confirmation.

CodexClaude CodeKimi CodeDeepSeek HarnessAntigravity

TaroBird 直接调用各家官方命令行工具，不重新实现智能体。在运行 Bot 的电脑上安装并登录任意一种即可；TaroBird 不附带 AI 引擎、模型账号或额度。TaroBird runs each vendor's official command-line agent instead of re-implementing it. Install and sign in to any of them on the computer that runs your Bots; TaroBird does not include AI engines, model accounts or credits.

TaroBird lets your phone and computers share the same Bots and conversations. Command-line agents run on a host that you install and control; the TaroBird apps are clients for that host. Optional Google connections let your configured tools work with mail, calendars and documents at your request. Google connections require separate setup and permission; browsing a Google website does not grant API access.TaroBird 让你的手机和电脑共用同一套 Bot 与对话。命令行智能体运行在你自己安装和控制的主机上，TaroBird 各端 App 都是这台主机的客户端。可选的 Google 连接可以让你配置的工具在你要求时处理邮件、日历和文档；Google 连接需要单独设置和授权，浏览 Google 网站本身不会授予 API 访问权限。
