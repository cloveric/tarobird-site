TaroBird Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 11, 2026

中文版

TaroBird is a set of apps (iOS, Android, macOS, Windows and a command-line host) for using AI agents that run on computers you control. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the TaroBird apps.

Summary

  • TaroBird has no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.
  • The developer of TaroBird does not operate a server that receives your conversations, files or device information. The apps talk only to the TaroBird host that you install and pair with.
  • Your conversations and files are stored on your own host and on the devices you pair with it.

Information stored on your devices and your host

When you pair a device, it creates a device key that stays on the device (in the iOS Keychain where available). Your host keeps the list of paired devices, your Bots, conversations, messages and attachments in its own database on your computer or server. You can remove a device from the host at any time and delete conversations in the app.

Network connections

The apps connect only to the host addresses in the connection details you scan or paste: for example a computer on your local network, a computer on your Tailscale network, or a relay server that you run. Connections use encryption pinned to your host's identity.

AI services you configure

Your host runs AI agents (for example command-line agents and models from providers such as OpenAI, Anthropic or DeepSeek) using accounts and API keys that you set up on the host. When you send a message to a Bot, your host may send that message and related files to the AI provider you chose. That processing is governed by the provider's own terms and privacy policy. The TaroBird developer does not receive this data.

Device permissions

The iOS app asks for permissions only when you use a feature that needs them:

  • Camera: to scan connection QR codes and take photos to attach to messages.
  • Photos: to choose images to attach, and to save a connection QR code if you ask.
  • Microphone: to record voice messages you send to a Bot.
  • Local network: to reach a host on your local network.
  • Face ID: to unlock the app if you turn on app lock.

Photos, recordings and files you attach are sent only to your host.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app registers with Apple Push Notification service (APNs) and gives the resulting device token to your host. Your host then sends notifications through APNs using credentials configured on that host. Apple handles the delivery under its own privacy policy.

Children

TaroBird is not directed to children under 13 and the developer does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/cloveric/tarobird/issues.

TaroBird 隐私政策（中文版）

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 11 日

TaroBird 是一组应用（iOS、Android、macOS、Windows 以及命令行主机），用来使用运行在你自己电脑上的 AI 智能体。本政策说明你使用 TaroBird 时信息如何处理。

概要

  • TaroBird 没有用户账号、没有广告、没有统计分析，也不做跟踪。
  • TaroBird 开发者不运营任何接收你对话、文件或设备信息的服务器。应用只和你自己安装并配对的 TaroBird 主机通信。
  • 你的对话和文件保存在你自己的主机和已配对的设备上。

保存在你设备和主机上的信息

配对时，设备会生成一把只保存在本机的设备密钥（iOS 上存入钥匙串）。你的主机在自己的数据库里保存已配对设备列表、Bot、对话、消息和附件。你可以随时在主机上移除设备，也可以在应用里删除对话。

网络连接

应用只连接你扫描或粘贴的连接信息里的主机地址，例如局域网里的电脑、你 Tailscale 网络里的电脑，或你自己运行的中转服务器。连接使用与主机身份绑定的加密。

你配置的 AI 服务

你的主机使用你在主机上配置的账号和 API 密钥运行 AI 智能体（例如 OpenAI、Anthropic、DeepSeek 等服务商的命令行智能体和模型）。你给 Bot 发消息时，主机可能会把消息和相关文件发给你选择的 AI 服务商处理，这部分适用服务商自己的条款和隐私政策。TaroBird 开发者不会收到这些数据。

设备权限

iOS 应用只在你使用相应功能时申请权限：相机（扫描连接二维码、拍照作为附件）、照片（选择图片作为附件、按你的要求保存连接二维码）、麦克风（录制发给 Bot 的语音消息）、本地网络（连接局域网里的主机）、面容 ID（开启应用锁后解锁）。你添加的照片、录音和文件只发送到你的主机。

通知

如果你允许通知，应用会向 Apple 推送通知服务（APNs）注册，并把得到的设备令牌交给你的主机。主机使用其上配置的凭据通过 APNs 发送通知，投递过程适用 Apple 的隐私政策。

儿童

TaroBird 不面向 13 岁以下儿童，开发者也不会有意收集儿童信息。

变更

本政策如有变更，会在本页面发布新版本并更新生效日期。

联系方式

关于本政策的问题，请在 github.com/cloveric/tarobird/issues 提交 issue。
