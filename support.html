Getting started

  1. Install and start the TaroBird desktop app (macOS or Windows) or the TaroBird host on your own computer or server. Downloads: github.com/cloveric/tarobird/releases.
  2. On a device that is already connected, open Devices & network › Add device to show a connection QR code.
  3. In the TaroBird iOS app, tap Scan connection QR code (or Paste connection details), then confirm the 8-digit pairing code on the connected device.

Common questions

The app cannot reach my host.

Your phone needs a network path to the host: the same Wi-Fi network, the same Tailscale network, or a relay server that you have set up. Check that the host is running and that its address in the connection details is reachable from the phone.

Do I need an account?

No. TaroBird has no accounts. Devices are paired with your host directly.

Where are my conversations stored?

On your host and on your paired devices. See the Privacy Policy.

Contact

Report a problem or ask a question by opening an issue at github.com/cloveric/tarobird/issues. Please do not include private conversation content, connection QR codes or connection links in public issues.

中文

开始使用

  1. 在你自己的电脑或服务器上安装并启动 TaroBird 桌面端（macOS / Windows）或 TaroBird 主机。下载地址：github.com/cloveric/tarobird/releases。
  2. 在已连接的设备上打开「设备与网络 › 添加设备」，显示连接二维码。
  3. 在 TaroBird iOS App 里点「扫描连接二维码」（或「粘贴连接信息」），然后在已连接的设备上确认 8 位配对码。

常见问题

连不上主机？手机需要能访问到主机：同一个 Wi-Fi、同一个 Tailscale 网络，或你自己部署的中转服务器。请确认主机在运行，并且连接信息里的地址手机能访问。

需要注册账号吗？不需要。TaroBird 没有账号体系，设备直接和你的主机配对。

对话存在哪里？存在你的主机和已配对的设备上，详见隐私政策。

联系我们

遇到问题请在 github.com/cloveric/tarobird/issues 提交 issue。公开 issue 里请不要贴对话内容、连接二维码或连接链接。
